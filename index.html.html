Built for life away from Earth

On a station or a Mars transit, food cannot be replaced on demand. Knowing what goes off first keeps menus on track.

Limited resupply

Every gram counts

Works offline

Nothing is sent to a server. The log stays on the device, and a saved copy of the page runs without internet.

The model adjusts for cabin, cold or freezer stowage, packaging (sealed pouch, freeze-dried, opened or rehydrated), and a higher spoilage factor for radiation-exposed environments.

How it works

Each check passes through the same six steps.

  1. Crew inputFood, stowage location, mission environment, time and quantity.
  2. Mission logEntries are timestamped against mission elapsed time.
  3. PreprocessingValues are converted to a common reference temperature.
  4. ML modelThe model weighs temperature, packaging and radiation exposure.
  5. Spoilage predictionFresh, Use Soon or Spoiled, with time remaining.
  6. Alert and menu priorityReminders 2 days ahead and an eat-first list before resupply.
